SAUDI ARABIA TRAVEL JOURNAL - CONTENT AND LAYOUT UPDATE

Open index.html in a modern browser or deploy the files to static hosting. The site uses standalone HTML pages with embedded CSS and vanilla JavaScript. No image folder or local photographs are used.

CURRENT STRUCTURE
- The project contains 24 HTML pages, including compatibility redirects for the eight consolidated nationality profiles.
- The homepage features Saudi eVisa information, an eight-country eligibility carousel, three destination stories (Riyadh, AlUla and Jeddah), Saudi food stories, and the mobile-app promotion.
- The Saudi Arabia eVisa Eligibility page contains the eight passport-specific profiles in the requested order; the former nationality URLs redirect to their matching country cards.
- Homepage carousel links open the relevant country card on the consolidated eligibility page. The old nationality URLs redirect to those same country-card anchors.
- Saudi food and cuisine has a dedicated article covering regional dishes, Saudi coffee and dates, breakfast, and traditional sweets.
- The five approved neighbouring-country links appear only on the dedicated nearby-countries page.

RESEARCH NOTES
- Food details were checked against Saudipedia's Saudi Cuisine and Saudi Coffee articles: https://saudipedia.com/en/saudi-cuisine and https://saudipedia.com/en/saudi-coffee . Jareesh is identified as the national dish, and maqshush as the national dessert; kabsa is described as a widely enjoyed dish, not the national dish.
- Visit Saudi's official travel guide covers practical planning topics including transport, communication and payments: https://www.visitsaudi.com/en/plan-your-trip/travel-guide .
- UNESCO's Saudi Arabia listing and Historic Jeddah entry were reviewed for heritage context: https://whc.unesco.org/en/statesparties/sa/ and https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/1361/ .
- Saudi eVisa eligibility and conditions can change. Check the current official terms and eligibility before publication or travel: https://visa.visitsaudi.com/ .
- The journal is independent third-party information, not a government service. Do not imply affiliation, guaranteed approval or guaranteed faster processing.

TECHNOLOGY AND DEPLOYMENT
- HTML5, inline CSS and vanilla JavaScript only.
- Canonical URLs and an absolute-URL XML sitemap should be added only after the journal's final hosting domain is confirmed. Do not use the promoted website's domain unless the journal is hosted there.
