Life Along Saudi Arabia’s Red Sea Coasts

Life along the Red Sea Coasts of Saudi Arabia moves at a slow and relaxed pace unlike those experienced in the other inland desert cities. The Red Sea runs over 1,700 kilometers with water that is clear and blue, surroundings covered with small sandy islands and old port towns.

Unique Sides of Saudi Arabia’s Red Sea

More than 1,000 Islands

Travellers who love spending time at peaceful beaches will have over 1,000 islands options to explore. Most of these islands have less crowd and clear turquoise waters.

Healthy Coral Reefs

Even in the warm water of the Red Sea, you’ll see that the coral reefs stay healthy, bright, colourful and are surrounded by hundreds of fish, dolphins and water species all year round.

Desert Sand Right Next to the Sea

On the northern side of the country, you can witness a surreal visual of the golden sand dunes rolling into the Red sea, making it one of the rare sights to experience.

Old Houses Made of Sea Coral

In the cities like Jeddah and Yanbu, you’ll see houses built with sea coral limestones and windows that are made of wood so that they could catch the cool sea breeze.

5 Coastal Places Worth Visiting

1. Umluj

Umluj is a beautiful coastal town, situated on the Northern Red Sea Coast which has a relaxed and peaceful vibe.

Top Things to Do

  • Boating: Take exciting boat trips to islands that are nearby such as Jabal Hassan Island and Umm Sahar Island.
  • Snorkelling & Scuba Diving: See the underwater life from up close, such as sea turtles, rays and fish.
  • Fishing & Dolphin Watching: You can try fishing in the Red Sea Coast and spot some dolphins also which completely depends on the season.
  • Farm Visits: Take a tour of Umluj’s local coastal farms which are famous for sweet mangoes and tall date palm trees.

2. Yanbu

This is the second largest Red Sea port city in Saudi Arabia, the first being Jeddah. Yanbu is certainly among the best spots to try water adventures and experience coastal life.

Top Things to Do

  • Shipwreck Diving: The best sites for scuba diving include Seven Sisters Reef and the 1914 SS Iona Wreck (for experienced divers).
  • Marina Cruises: You can go to Sharm Yanbu marina as boat departures take place from here. Enjoy the scenic sunset views.
  • Relax by the Beach: Yanbu Al-Bahr and Yanbu Beach are perfect relaxation spots for travellers looking for a calm break.
  • Visit Waterfront Parks: Choose Yanbu Industrial Waterfront & Corniche, Yanbu Lake Park & Al Fairouz Park for a relaxed weekend outing with your friends or family.

3. Jeddah

Jeddah is among the major coastal hubs in Saudi Arabia offering tourists to see modern waterfronts, white sandy beaches and iconic marinas. Al-Balad is a UNESCO World Heritage spot that must not be missed visiting.

Top Things to Do

  • Visit Popular Waterfronts: The Jeddah Corniche & Waterfront for cool sea breeze and soothing views of King Fahd’s Fountain.
  • Explore the Floating Mosque: Visit the Al-Rahmah Mosque built on stilts over the Red Sea which make it appear as if floating during high tide.
  • Hop Between Beaches: Relax at the Silver Sands Beach, Obhur Bay or just take a short boat trip to Bayada Island.
  • Eat Fresh Sea Food: Eat freshly cooked fish of the Red Sea at the Central Fish Market or at harbourside restaurant.

4. Farasan Islands

The Farasan islands are a protected group of coral islands and are located on the Southern Red Sea Coast of Saudi Arabia. You can reach here by taking a ferry from the Jazan Port.

Top Things to Do

  • Beach Camping: Enjoy camping along the Red sea in spots like Abrah Beach.
  • Coral Reef Diving: dive into the clear sea waters of Red Sea to see vibrant coral reefs and sea turtles along and other aquatic wildlife.
  • Cruise through Mangroves: Take the popular Al-Qandal boat route to spot the rare migratory birds.
  • Attend Seasonal Events: Be a part of Hareed (Parrotfish) Festival and enjoy traditional folk music, dance and fishing competitions.

5. The Red Sea Islands

The Red Sea Islands are located between Umluj and Al-Wajh. These islands are calm, and most resorts here run on solar power which makes them nature-friendly.

Top Things to Do

  • Water Activities: Engage in water sport adventures like scuba diving and snorkelling and get a chance to see Hawksbill sea turtles, Reef fish and Halavi guitarfish.
  • Play Golf on Island: Play island golf at Shura Links on Shura Island, this is the very first island golf course in Saudi Arabia.
  • Beachfront and Over Water Stays: Enjoy luxury stays at islands like Shebara, known for modern houses built over the Sea.

Get Ready for Your Sea Trip

  1. Keep Travel Documents: Keep your passport and Saudi Arabia eVisa (if applicable) with you at all times.
  2. Prepare Your Itinerary: Include destinations and activities in your itinerary that match your preferences and plan how you’ll execute those.
  3. Choose Certified Operators: For any water sport adventure or a boat cruise, book with a certified and reliable booking tour operator to ensure safety and top-notch experience.
  4. Follow the Schedule: Always check the opening and closing hours of beaches, parks and activities as some of these close early and have different operational hours during weekends.
  5. Be a Responsible Traveller: Do not leave trash behind or never touch or damage any coral reefs. Give respect to locals and other visitors.
