Explore the Sandstone Landscapes of AlUla

AlUla is a place of unique sandstone formations, varied desert scenery and several sites that hold archaeological and cultural importance.

Discover the story

Quick Trip Planning

For first-time visitors travelling to AlUla, make notes of these essential details:

  • What to Get: For travelling to AlUla or any place in the country, you must get a Saudi eVisa (if eligible) and enjoy exploring the country.
  • How to Get: You can easily apply for Saudi eVisa online for you and your family and obtain it before leaving your home country.
  • When to Visit: October to March is generally the best time to visit AlUla as it offers pleasant day time temperatures, ideal for day tours.
  • How to Travel Around: Either rent a car, take a bus or AlUla's hop-on/hop-off tourist shuttles , whatever suits your budget and convenience.

The Story Behind Rock Formations of AlUla

Once Underwater

The dramatic rock formations that you see today in AlUla were actually shaped by nature. Millions of years ago, this area was underwater. There came a time when it all dried up, and the strong winds and rain gradually formed the giant cliffs, pillars, and arches.

Nature’s Lightshow

The AlUla rocks contain iron minerals which make them appear to change colours during each time of the day. In the morning the rocks look light gold, at noon they turn bright orange and as soon as the sun sets they appear deep red.

Early Settlers

People settled in AlUla around 2,000 years ago because of its fresh oasis water. They carved massive tombs and thousands of inscriptions and messages into the rock walls.

4 Places You Can’t Miss Visiting

1. Hegra

Hegra is popular among tourists for being an archaeological spot. It has over 100 desert tombs which are preserved and are carved into huge boulders.

Why Visit: It’s the first UNESCO World Heritage Site in Saudi Arabia. The same civilisation built it that created Petra in Jordan.

2. Elephant Rock

Elephant rock is a 52-meter huge natural rock formation that resembles an elephant which was shaped naturally, millions of years ago by wind and water erosion.

Why Visit: To experience one of the best sunsets and cosy relaxation in sunken firepit lounges.

3. Jabal Ikmah

Jabal Ikmah is a narrow desert canyon covered in hundreds of ancient rock carvings and historic inscriptions.

Why Visit: It’s an open air library which has the world’s biggest, well-preserved collection of Dadanitic and Lihyanite rock inscriptions.

4. Dadan

Dadan is a popular ancient historic city which is famous for once being the capital of two powerful desert kingdoms, the Dadan and Lihyan.

Why Visit: See the iconic lion tombs that are carved into red rock cliffs, explore the 2,000 year old city and see the ancient stories carved on stones.

3 Ways to Experience the Landscape

Star Watching at Gharameel

Al Gharameel Nature Reserve is a specially designated protected area in AlUla which is famous for being the first certified Dark Sky Park in the Middle East.

On a guided night tour, travellers, especially astronomy lovers can easily see the Milky Way, different star patterns, planets without any blocked view by bright city lights.

Harrat Viewpoint at Sunset

Take around a 30-minute drive up to this high volcanic plateau, giving you breathtaking 360 degree views of AlUla’s sandstone cliffs, oasis farms, mud brick villages and old town.

Enjoy the stunning sunset views and a scrumptious dinner while the town lights make your surroundings glow.

Hiking & 4x4 Safari Rides

For adventurous travellers, a jeep ride is a great activity to see the red sand dunes and explore the desert.

Travellers who are into hiking can hike up the sandy trails and watch the unique shapes from close. Some popular spots include Sharaan Nature Reserve and Rainbow Rock.

What to Pack for Your AlUla Trip

Comfortable Shoes

Pack a walking shoe which must be fully covered and must have a good grip to walk on sand dunes, uneven rocks or hike up the trails.

Sun Protection

You must bring a good sunscreen with a high SPF, a light coloured stole, sunglasses, and a wide brim hat as protection against sun.

Warm Layers

The temperature during night can drop significantly and can be breezy, so carrying a light jacket, sweater or a warm shawl is a must.

Light Breathable Clothes

Daytime can be hot so carrying light breathable clothes that are modest will make your trip more comfortable and enjoyable.

Reusable Water Bottles

Desert exploration during day or night can make you thirsty and that too frequently so carry a reusable water bottle that can be refilled easily.

Travel Documents

Keep your passport and a printed and digital copy of your eVisa (if applicable) in a small bag that stays with you at all times.
